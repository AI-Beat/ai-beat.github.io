# AI Headlines — 2026-10-03

- [Scalable decision-making for games of imperfect information](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11036-y) — MIT/CMU/NYU/Stanford researchers publish Ataraxos in Nature: the first AI to play Stratego at a superhuman level, beating the four-time world champion 15-1-4, trained for under $8,000 using self-play RL and a generative model for decision-time belief tracking. *(September 30, 2026)*

- [FLUX 3 Image](https://bfl.ai/models/flux-3-image) — Black Forest Labs releases the image component of its multimodal FLUX 3 system: bounding-box composition across a 0–1000 normalized canvas, multi-step editing that leaves untouched regions alone, up to 10 reference images, and native 4K output; open weights announced for coming weeks. *(October 1, 2026)*

- [DwarfStar 4 (ds4)](https://dwarfstar.sh/) — antirez (creator of Redis) ships an updated local inference engine in C that now supports DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, GLM 5.x, and Qwen3.8 Flash Next with asymmetric 2-bit quantization and KV cache stored to SSD; targets 64GB+ Apple Silicon, NVIDIA, and AMD ROCm machines. *(September 2026)*

- [Muse Gadgets](https://gadgets.muse.ai) — Muse AI open-sources SDKs for ESP32, Raspberry Pi, and Linux boards to build physical hardware gadgets that talk to the Muse AI assistant; first-party Home Link device ships free to US subscribers in October. *(October 2026)*
