# AI Headlines — 2026-10-30

- [Mistral Releases Open Weights for Mistral Large 4 (Le Chonk)](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4-oss/) — A 1-trillion-parameter MoE model with ~52B active parameters drops as open weights under a modified MIT license (with a MaaS carveout); claims top open-weight scores on FrontierCode 2.0, Terminal-Bench 4.0, and the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index using 47% fewer tokens per task. *(October 30, 2026)*

- [We ran AI agent swarms in Minecraft and Mindustry](https://labs.ycombinator.com/projects/games/index.html) — YC Labs ran 50 GPT-6.1 Sol agents in a Minecraft survival world; within three hours they invented a pseudo-government, ration ledger, and the "sole fertilizer operator" role — and the emergent risk aversion and role fixation mirror patterns the authors see in coding agents. *(October 30, 2026)*

- [A tiny world model running games on a Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W](https://github.com/SheepSeb/tinyworldformer) — An int8-quantized transformer world model with 26k–131k parameters runs fully on the RP2350's 520 KB SRAM; a tiny MLP actor then plays Breakout, Pac-Man lite, and three other games by sampling from the model's hallucinated frames. *(October 2026)*
