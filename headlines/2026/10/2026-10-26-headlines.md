# AI Headlines — 2026-10-26

- [A 10-Year-Old CNN Outperforms Frontier LLMs for Skin Cancer Diagnosis](https://lynchlab.net/llm-skin-cancer) — Dermatologist Magnus Lynch compared fine-tuned Inception v3 against GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable 5.1 on 200 biopsy-proven skin lesions; the CNN scored 62% accuracy vs ~42% for both LLMs, with a large advantage on correctly recognising harmless lesions. *(October 25, 2026)*

- [The State of Context Engineering](https://www.dbreunig.com/2026/10/24/the-state-of-context-engineering.html) — Drew Breunig traces the evolution from prompt engineering to context engineering to harness engineering, catalogues the failure modes each step introduced, and argues that persistent agents will require "strategic forgetting" to stay functional. *(October 24, 2026)*

- [OpenAI and Anthropic Cut LLM Prices Up to 80%: Why?](https://minimaxir.com/2026/10/cheap-llms/) — Max Woolf documents the wave of API price cuts across 2026 and argues open-weight competition, inference efficiency gains, and commoditisation pressure are driving them — while noting the math looks awkward in an IPO prospectus. *(October 25, 2026)*
