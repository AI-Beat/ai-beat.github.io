- [Redesigning Source Control at Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/redesigning-source-control) — Anthropic's monorepo hit 30,000 merges/day by September (up from 2,500 in May), most from AI agents; a seven-person team built Scion, a centralized VCS that uses 200× less CPU/memory than their old git mirroring setup, in five months. *(October 14, 2026)*

- [AlphaNIST: Robot Assembly from Sparse Reward](https://alpha-nist.github.io/) — Preprint (arXiv:2610.13354) shows a single RL policy trained with only binary success/fail signal — no demonstrations, no progress shaping — reaching 98.2% success across all 20 NIST Task Board assembly tasks in simulation, with emergent re-grasping and reorientation behaviors. *(October 2026)*

- [Is 'Attention' the Essence of Intelligence?](https://www.quantamagazine.org/is-attention-the-essence-of-intelligence-20261012/) — Quanta Magazine traces attention from Bahdanau's 2014 translation fix through the transformer and into modern LLMs, asking whether next-word prediction is a working definition of intelligence. *(October 12, 2026)*

- [I posted to HN, and then my website was flooded with AI bot traffic](https://www.sammystraus.com/#i-posted-to-hn-and-then-my-website-was-flooded-with-ai-bot-traffic) — Developer discovers that after a HN post, nearly half the traffic came from a single AI newsletter-summarization crawler; uses NGINX logs to identify and block it alongside other AI scanners. *(October 14, 2026)*
