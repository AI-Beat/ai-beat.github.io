# AI Headlines — 2026-10-28

- [Genie 4: A New Generation of World Simulation](https://deepmind.google/blog/genie-4-a-new-generation-of-world-simulation/) — DeepMind's real-time interactive world model generates physics-accurate environments from text prompts, now with native audio; it uses Gemini Omni for visual anchoring and Gemini 3.8 Flash as a steering agent, and is positioned for robotics and agent training. *(October 27, 2026)*

- [Introducing LFM3: Every Modality for Specialized Intelligence](https://www.liquid.ai/blog/lfm3) — Liquid AI releases six open-weight models (text, vision, audio, omni, and two decision variants) all built from a single 1.7B-parameter base, fitting in ~1 GB at 4-bit, with a 256K context window and a claim of outperforming the larger LFM2.5-2.6B. *(October 27, 2026)*

- [Survival LLM: AI assistant and emergency library that runs on your own computer](https://www.brangus.io/survival-llm) — An offline-first AI assistant bundled with ~930,000 Wikipedia articles, medical references, FEMA guidance, and U.S. military survival manuals; selects a model tier (Nemotron to Granite 8B) based on available RAM, runs fully locally after initial setup on macOS or Windows. *(October 26–27, 2026)*
