# AI Headlines — 2026-09-02

- [Slotstream: run Qwen3.8-Flash-Next on a Mac by streaming MoE experts from SSD](https://github.com/carloslfu/slotstream) — Swift/MLX tool that keeps the 3.8 GB dense trunk in RAM and pages the 68 GB of routed experts from NVMe SSD into a fixed cache-slot pool, making a 104 GB 4-bit MoE model runnable on Apple Silicon with far less than 104 GB of RAM. *(September 1, 2026)*

- [The Emergent Symbolic Structure of Artificial Neural Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29530) — McCoy, Soulos, Linzen, and Smolensky show that neural network representations can be closely approximated by closed-form symbolic equations, and that targeted interventions on those structures reliably shift LLM behavior — from small list-manipulation networks up to large models handling arithmetic, logic, code, and language. *(August 30, 2026)*

- [The efficient frontier of LLM inference](https://www.baseten.co/blog/the-efficient-frontier-of-llm-inference/) — Baseten's primer distinguishes tradeoff-management techniques (batch size, parallelism, quantization) from frontier-expanding ones (kernel fusion, speculative decoding, P/D disaggregation), and shows why the frontier is "jagged" and must be found empirically rather than analytically. *(September 1, 2026)*

- [Atlas: A World Model for Spatial Intelligence](https://www.worldlabs.ai/blog/atlas) — World Labs releases Atlas, a multimodal autoregressive diffusion transformer pretrained from scratch on text, images, video, and 3D; supports camera-controlled generation up to 1440p/1 minute, spatial reconstruction from one to dozens of images, and novel view synthesis. *(September 1, 2026)*
