# AI Headlines — 2026-09-16

- [Introducing System One Models and Jev](https://typesafe.ai/blog/introducing-system-one-models-and-jev) — TypeSafe AI ships Jev, a model that abandons text generation entirely in favor of parallel typed probabilistic decisions with calibrated confidence scores; 40–200x faster and two orders of magnitude cheaper than LLMs on structured automation tasks. *(September 15, 2026)*

- [Learning to Solve Hard Problems in RL for LLMs by Never Giving Up](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13443) — New paper identifies the "Matthew Effect" in GRPO training—easy problems improve while hard ones stagnate—and proposes Never Give Up (NGU), an adaptive sampling strategy that keeps generating completions until it finds one correct, redistributing compute toward the hard tail. *(September 11, 2026)*

- [Stay discoverable in search while disallowing AI training](https://blog.cloudflare.com/accountable-mixed-use-ai-crawlers/) — Cloudflare launches controls allowing site owners to block AI training crawlers from Accountable operators (Apple, Google, Microsoft) while staying indexed for search, decoupling training from discoverability for the first time. *(September 15, 2026)*
