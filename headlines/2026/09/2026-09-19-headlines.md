- [Cactus Needle 3: 9MB On-Device Automation Model](https://cactuscompute.com/needle) — YC S25 startup Cactus Compute ships Needle 3, a Laddered Simple Attention Network with Monarch Hadamard MLPs that fits in 8–29 MB, beats models 10× its size on mobile tool-calling, and matches models 2–3× its size on structured extraction; Apache 2.0. *(September 19, 2026)*

- [OpenAI Used Its Own LLMs to Design Jalapeño Chip](https://spectrum.ieee.org/llms-for-chip-design) — IEEE Spectrum piece on how OpenAI's <100-person hardware team used LLMs for frontend design and HLS, improving a benchmark from 0.31% to 88.94% of theoretical capacity in 40 hours, and achieving first RTL to tape-out in 9 months. *(September 14, 2026)*

- [135+ Projects Are Already Using Jev to Gate Agent Tool Calls](https://github.com/yibie/awesome-jev) — Four days after launch, developers are wiring TypeSafe's Jev decision model into agent harnesses as a sub-500ms, near-free gate before every side-effecting tool call; five open-source clones (OpenJev, mini-jev, jevlike, MLX constrained decoding, a vLLM PR) have appeared, all reading logits directly but none yet reproducing RLCD calibration. *(September 15–19, 2026)*
