# AI Headlines — 2026-09-09

- [Mercury 2.5](https://www.inceptionlabs.ai/blog/introducing-mercury-2-5) — InceptionLabs releases its latest diffusion language model doing 1,107 tokens/second on standard NVIDIA GPUs; 260K context, parallel tool calls, and launch pricing at $0.20/$0.75/M tokens. Voice agent OpenCall reports P99 latency dropped from minutes to 1 second. *(September 8, 2026)*

- [Deltafin: Kimi K3 (2.8T) at 1 token/s on a MacBook Pro, streamed from four SSDs](https://github.com/argonautlabsai/deltafin) — A Rust-based inference engine streams 1.45TB of Kimi K3 expert weights from external SSDs and achieves ~1.0 tok/s on an M5 Max with 128GB RAM; progress from 0.014 tok/s in July shows what software optimization can do against a fixed hardware budget. *(September 8, 2026)*

- [AlphaGenome Atlas: A High-Resolution Map of Human DNA](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/alphagenome-atlas/) — Google DeepMind pre-computed predictions for all 9 billion possible single-nucleotide variants using AlphaGenome, producing a 1-petabyte dataset; a web portal exposes the AlphaGenome Variant Impact (AVI) score with no coding required, addressing the 98% of the genome that isn't protein-coding. *(September 8, 2026)*

- [On the Navier–Stokes Millennium Prize Problem](https://openai.com/index/navier-stokes-solution/) — OpenAI reports an internal model produced a Lean-verified proof of Navier-Stokes existence and smoothness using ~10,000 parallel agents over 88 hours; the work originated after hearing rumors of related progress by researchers Levent Alpöge and Tristan Buckmaster, whose own proof addressed the forced Euler equations rather than Navier-Stokes. *(September 8, 2026)*
