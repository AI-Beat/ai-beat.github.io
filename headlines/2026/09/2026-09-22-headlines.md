- [MiMo-V2.6-Pro and Flash: Xiaomi Open-Sources Frontier-Competitive Omnimodal Models](https://mimo.mi.com/docs/en-US/updates/model) — Xiaomi releases two MIT-licensed sparse MoE models (1.02T/42B active and 309B/15B active) that score ahead of Kimi K3 on open-weight benchmarks and on par with Claude Opus 5 on agent evals; RL post-training pushed DeepSWE from 58.4 to 72.57 on Pro. *(September 21–22, 2026)*

- [AI Coding Has Made CI a Bottleneck, So We Reworked Ours to Keep Up](https://linear.app/now/ci-bottleneck-reworked) — Linear's engineering team details how quadrupling AI-assisted test coverage broke their CI pipeline and how they responded: switching runners, sparse checkouts, batching jobs, and resharding tests to cut per-test runner time in half and save 87,000 runner-minutes per month. *(September 22, 2026)*

- [Frontier AI on Your Own Hardware — DLab Open Source Week](https://timdettmers.com/2026/09/21/dlab-open-source-week/) — Tim Dettmers' academic lab releases Agent Harness, an Efficient Inference Framework for running quantized frontier models on a single 24GB GPU, and CliffCompaction, an auto-compaction technique that cuts long-running agent context costs by ~50%; claims the resulting autonomous research system beats frontier deep research tools. *(September 21, 2026)*
