# AI Headlines — 2026-09-30

- [Frontier Learning: Training LLM Reasoners at the Edge of Capability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35426) — Post-training method that uses procedural generators and a regret signal to continuously produce problems at the edge of the model's current capability; 115% relative gain over fixed-pool baselines on a probability reasoning task. *(September 28, 2026)*

- [AGMAI: Responsible Release of AI-Generated Mathematics](https://agmai.org/general-sep29/) — Statement drawing on 600+ survey responses from mathematicians; distinguishes results humans understand from those they don't, and places explicit responsibility on AI labs to fund community comprehension of opaque AI-generated proofs. *(September 29, 2026)*

- [PSSA: A non-transformer language model written from scratch in Rust](https://github.com/Sparticle62ops/pssa) — Combines a selective state-space recurrence, a hyperbolic episodic memory bank, and plastic weights that update during inference; claims 12× faster generation than a transformer at matched parameters on CPU; HN discussion is skeptical of the evaluation methodology. *(September 30, 2026)*
