# AI Headlines — 2026-09-17

- [Training a 4B model to produce 81% faster query plans than Postgres](https://rohanbansal.com/qorl) — Rohan Bansal trains a 4B model with off-policy distillation from GPT-6 Astra then anchored GRPO RL to generate Postgres join-order hints, achieving 1.81× geometric mean speedup on 113 JOB queries for ~$1,200 total compute. *(September 16, 2026)*

- [Breaking the 1.58-bit Barrier for Ternary LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16338) — Intel researchers find zeros account for up to 51.5% of ternary LLM weights, making standard five-trit packing suboptimal; their BITCOS format (presence bitmap + sign vector) reaches 1.485 bits/weight and delivers 1.10–1.28× decode speedup on Intel CPUs and GPUs. *(September 14, 2026)*

- [HarnessTax: How Much Does the Harness Matter for Coding Agents?](https://harnesstax.github.io/) — Empirical study showing the agent harness matters substantially: the same model with different harnesses can differ by 32× in per-task cost on SWE-bench Lite, with context consumption mattering more than raw call count. *(September 2026)*

- [Dream-RSI: Recursive Self-Improvement through Evolving Worlds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14858) — Orchestration framework that converts historical discovery trees into a replay simulator for off-policy feedback, enabling continuous self-improvement of exploration policies across algorithm engineering, math optimization, and GPU kernel engineering. *(September 14, 2026)*
