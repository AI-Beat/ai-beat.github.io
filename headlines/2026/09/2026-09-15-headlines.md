# AI Headlines — 2026-09-15

- [Pion, an agent designed to run any company autonomously](https://andonlabs.com/pion) — Andon Labs opens a research-preview cloud platform where persistent AI agents operate real businesses — a retail store in SF, a café in Stockholm, and vending machines — finding models improving qualitatively but not yet profitable. *(September 14, 2026)*

- [Why don't machine learning research agents overfit?](https://www.amazon.science/blog/why-dont-machine-learning-research-agents-overfit) — Amazon Science shows that successful ML research strategies compress down to 16–32 tokens via an information-bottleneck test, proving that agents are learning genuine patterns rather than memorizing validation sets. *(September 10, 2026)*

- [Backprop Alternative: Augmented Lagrangian Predictive Coding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.31022) — Sakana AI's PC-ALM uses layer-local Lagrange multipliers to train residual MLPs up to 1000 layers without backpropagation, nearly matching BP performance especially in deep narrow networks. *(May 2026)*
