# AI Headlines — 2026-11-05

- [Ripple-65B Preview: U.S. Open-Weight SOTA on Agentic Coding](https://www.ricursive.com/ripple) — Ricursive Intelligence releases a 65B dense model that scores 80.52 on TerminalBench 2.1, beating models up to 816B parameters; total R&D cost was ~$32M, one-tenth of the previous open-weight SOTA, with ~70x fewer RL rollouts. *(November 4, 2026)*

- [Progress on Provable Inference for Large Language Models](https://www.anthropic.com/research/provable-inference) — Anthropic and collaborators from EPFL, MIT, StarkWare, and Succinct release FlashProve, an open-source zkSNARK-based system that lets users cryptographically verify which model weights produced a response; runs at ~110 tokens/sec on a single H100, with ~70–95x GPU overhead versus standard inference. *(November 2, 2026)*

- [Kimi K3.1 Pro weights released on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/moonshotai/Kimi-K3.1-Pro) — Moonshot AI open-weights Kimi K3.1 Pro, a 2.8T-parameter MoE with 104B active parameters, 1M context, and native multimodal (text + vision) support; post-trained from Kimi K3 with quantization-aware training (MXFP4/MXFP8). *(November 4–5, 2026)*
