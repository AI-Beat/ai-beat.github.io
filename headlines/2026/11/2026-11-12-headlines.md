# AI Headlines — 2026-11-12

- [United Airlines bans AI agents from booking flights on behalf of travelers](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/united-airlines-bans-ai-agents-booking-flights/) — United updated its ticketing policy to prohibit third-party personal AI agents, citing security, unintended actions, and federal luggage-declaration rules; Southwest takes the opposite stance, keeping inventory open to ChatGPT users. *(November 11, 2026)*

- [Can a Linear Program Do Weight-Space Programming?](https://nathan.rs/posts/weight-space-lp/) — Nathan Barry shows that GPT-6 Astra's 8-hour weight-editing search is a linear program in disguise; an LP solves the same problem in ~1 minute with 8× fewer weight changes and matching accuracy on GSM8K, MATH-500, and IFEval. *(November 9, 2026)*

- [Gemini Decisions API](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-decisions-api/) — New Google API that evaluates up to 1,000 yes/no, multiple-choice, and rubric questions in a single forward pass against one multimodal input (up to 256K tokens); 150–250 ms end-to-end, $0.10/M input tokens. *(November 11, 2026)*

- [Huawei releases MACaQ, its internal quantizer for local inference](https://github.com/huawei-csl/MACaQ) — Apache 2.0 toolkit quantizes 122B-parameter models in 64 minutes with 32.5 GB memory; MACaQ-R refines existing GGUF weight codes without reformatting, running on stock llama.cpp. *(November 2026)*
