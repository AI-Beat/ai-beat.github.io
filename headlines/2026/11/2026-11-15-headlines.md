- [Advancing science and engineering with Antigravity's /teamwork feature](https://research.google/blog/advancing-science-engineering-with-antigravitys-teamwork-feature/) — Google Research describes how coordinated Gemini 4 Argon agents (via Antigravity's /teamwork) proved the Courtade-Kumar conjecture, produced a 1.8M-line Lean formalization of the Weil conjectures, hit 95% top-1 on NMR structure determination, and rewrote libgav1 from C++ to Rust 20–30% faster than the original. *(November 13, 2026)*

- [The Surprising Effectiveness of Deleting Weights in LLM Post-Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10866) — Jack Lu et al. show that Bit-Mask Tuning (BMT)—learning which gate-projection weights to zero, with no value updates—matches full fine-tuning across GRPO, SFT, and on-policy distillation while using thousands of times less storage and forgetting far less. *(November 7, 2026 / NeurIPS 2026)*

- [Lovable apps expose passport details and 200K+ private messages](https://www.crissyfield.de/research/lovable-en/) — Crissy Field GmbH analyzed 159K Lovable projects from Common Crawl; 24,664 sent database records at page load with no authentication, and further reads in 30% of Supabase-backed sites turned up email addresses, with examples including 80K profiles, 200K chat messages, and passport records. *(~November 2026)*
