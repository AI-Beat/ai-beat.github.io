# AI Headlines — 2026-11-21

- [Frostbite: A new CPU architecture discovered, designed and verified by AI](https://architectlabs.com/blog/frostbite) — Architect Labs claims AI agents autonomously designed a CPU using interaction nets from scratch, 4.59× performance-per-watt vs. a conventional in-order core across 25 workloads; RTL, ISA, and 800k+ Lean proofs all agent-generated. *(November 18, 2026)*

- [Language Models Can Rewrite Their Own Weights](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21885) — A 27B model diagnoses its own failures and applies gradient-free weight edits over eight iterative sessions, raising accuracy 6.6 points on unseen problems and recovering >80% performance on a damaged copy without reading the original weights. *(November 16–18, 2026)*

- [AI apparently proves the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis with 17.4 million lines of Lean code](https://scottaaronson.blog/?p=10344) — Scott Aaronson reports that Anthropic models produced a claimed proof of GRH with Lean verification; he expects the result to hold but notes mathematicians will need years to absorb the ideas. *(November 20, 2026)*

- [Reson8 open-sources Rumble-2-Flash, a 0.5B speech recognition model for 40+ languages](https://huggingface.co/Reson8/Rumble-2-Flash) — Apache 2.0 weights covering 40+ languages; company-reported 17.6% average WER on 10 lower-resource languages versus 39.8% for Whisper Large V3; custom vocabulary stays in the closed API tier. *(November 18, 2026)*

- [Language Models Make New Research Sound Old](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23546) — Six frontier LLMs reported open research questions as partly studied in 30–90% of cases where they knew the question was open; a structured checking pipeline raised clear reporting from 8% to 95% for one model. *(November 17, 2026)*
