- [AI is dramatically accelerating AI research](https://www.planned-obsolescence.org/p/ai-is-dramatically-accelerating-ai) — Ajeya Cotra reads public evidence from Anthropic's S-1/A and a METR report to argue that the RSP threshold for AI-attributable research acceleration (~2×) has likely been crossed at Anthropic, with industry safeguards far behind. *(November 6, 2026)*

- [A Jev that can ask Opus for help gives Opus accuracy at 4x lower cost](https://hightouch.com/blog/llm-judges-identity-resolution) — Hightouch builds a cascade LLM judge for identity resolution: a cheap calibrated model screens all cases and escalates uncertain ones to Opus; the key insight is that calibration beats raw accuracy for effective routing. *(November 4, 2026)*

- [Cognition launches SWE-2 Flash in Devin at one-fifth the price of SWE-2](https://runtimewire.com/article/cognition-swe-2-flash-devin-launch) — Built on Thinking Machines' Inkling-Small (276B/12B-active MoE), 3× faster and ~80% cheaper than SWE-2; available in Devin Desktop and CLI, weights not public. *(November 4, 2026)*

- [Outbound SIP calling now available to all OpenAI API organizations](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/voice-sip) — OpenAI's voice models can now initiate outbound phone calls via SIP trunking; sideband WebSocket lets backends receive events, run tools, and send commands while SIP carries audio. *(November 7, 2026)*
