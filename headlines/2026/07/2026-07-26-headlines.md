# AI Headlines — 2026-07-26

- [Running a 28.9M parameter LLM on an $8 microcontroller](https://github.com/slvDev/esp32-ai) — A new project runs a 28.9M-parameter LLM at 9.5 tok/s on an ESP32-S3 by keeping the 25M-param embedding table in flash and loading only needed rows into SRAM — making the per-layer embeddings trick from Gemma work on hardware with 512KB of RAM. *(July 26, 2026)*

- [Inflect-Micro-v2: complete voice synthesis in 9.36M parameters](https://huggingface.co/owensong/Inflect-Micro-v2) — An independent developer's VITS-family TTS model fits under 10M parameters, runs at 6.28× real-time on a 4-thread CPU, and achieves 66.2% community preference over established baselines; Apache-2.0. *(July 26, 2026)*

- [General Resolution: LLM usage in Debian](https://www.debian.org/vote/2026/vote_002) — Debian developers are voting on three competing proposals for AI-assisted contributions: a full ban, a regulated-permission framework, and a cultural-discouragement policy — each reflecting a genuinely different theory of what the risk actually is. *(July 24, 2026)*
