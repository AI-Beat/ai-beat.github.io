# AI Headlines — 2026-07-28

- [Our position on open-weights models](https://www.anthropic.com/news/position-open-weights-models) — Anthropic breaks its silence as the last major lab not to sign the open-weight defense letter: Dario Amodei says they've never supported a ban, but wants mandatory safety testing for capable models, tighter chip export controls, and a crackdown on what he calls "industrial-scale distillation operations" where actors extract frontier model knowledge into open-weight form. *(July 27, 2026)*

- [A $500 RL fine-tune of a 9B open model beat frontier models on catalog review](https://fermisense.com/when-machines-take-the-wheel/) — Fermi Sense and Ramp used GRPO to fine-tune a 9B open-source model on a catalog review task for $500; the result outperformed every frontier configuration tested at 87.3% vs 76.9%, at roughly 40x lower inference cost — raising real questions about when task-specific RL beats expensive generalist APIs. *(July 27, 2026)*

- [Neutrino-1 8B](https://fermionresearch.com/models/neutrino-8b/) — Fermion Research releases a ternary-family weight format 8B model that compresses to 3.88 GB (roughly one-eighth of fp16 size) while running at 396 tok/s on an H100 and 33.7 tok/s on an Apple M5; based on Qwen3-8B, Apache 2.0, single deployable artifact across GPU and CPU hardware. *(July 27, 2026)*

- [Using an open model feels surprisingly good](https://matthewsaltz.com/blog/using-an-open-model-feels-surprisingly-good/) — A developer deploys Kimi K3 via Modal and points their editor at the personal endpoint; describes an unexpected sense of data ownership and autonomy that comes from routing inference through your own infrastructure rather than a third-party API. *(July 28, 2026)*
